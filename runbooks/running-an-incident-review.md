# Runbook: Running an incident review

_Last reviewed: 2026-11-22_

## When to use this
Use this runbook when the situation in the title applies to a service owned by this repository.

## Steps
1. Collect the timeline from the incident channel.
2. Draft the review within three working days.
3. Hold a blameless review meeting.
4. Track follow-up actions as issues.

## Escalation
If the steps do not resolve it, page the on-call engineer.
