# Runbook: Onboarding a new engineer

_Last reviewed: 2026-11-16_

## When to use this
Use this runbook when the situation in the title applies to a service owned by this repository.

## Steps
1. Create accounts and add them to the team.
2. Share the onboarding guide.
3. Pair on a first small change.
4. Schedule a check-in after the first week.

## Escalation
If the steps do not resolve it, page the on-call engineer.
